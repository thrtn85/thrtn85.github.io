      a: Enough to get it right. We build in review rounds and tell you up front how that works, so you're never surprised. The goal is a brand you're genuinely happy with, not a rushed one.

      a: Absolutely. Since we also build websites, we can carry your new look straight through to your site, so everything lines up without a handoff between two different teams.
