    - q: We're small. Do we really need a help center?
      a: Even a handful of well-written answers can save you hours and make customers feel cared for. We'll start with what fits your size. Sometimes that's a few solid articles, not a whole system.
    - q: Do we have to buy expensive software?
      a: No. We recommend tools that match your needs and budget, including free and low-cost options. The goal is the right fit, not the biggest invoice.
    - q: Will this replace the personal touch?
      a: Just the opposite. By handling the repetitive questions automatically, your team has more time for the conversations that really need a human. The setup supports your people; it doesn't replace them.
    - q: Can you work with the tools we already use?
      a: In most cases, yes. We'll look at what you have first and build on it where it makes sense, so you're not starting over or learning everything from scratch.
    - q: How do you write the help articles?
      a: We start with the questions your customers actually ask, then write clear answers in your voice. You review everything before it goes live, so it always sounds like you.
    - q: What if our team won't use the new system?
      a: That's why we design it around how they already work and walk them through it together. A setup only counts as done when your team is comfortable using it day to day.
