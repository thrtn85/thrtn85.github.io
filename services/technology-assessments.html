The Overview

A Checkup for Your Technology

A technology assessment is a thorough look at the tools, systems, and processes your organization runs on. We review your website, your software, your security, and the way work moves through your team, then hand you a plain-language report on what we found and what to do about it.

It's the right starting point if something feels off but you can't quite name it. Maybe your site is slow, you're worried about security, you suspect you're overpaying for tools, or things just take longer than they should. Instead of guessing, you get a clear picture and a prioritized list.

The value is in the clarity. You'll know what's genuinely urgent, what can wait, and what isn't worth worrying about, so you can spend your time and money where they'll actually make a difference. There's no obligation to have us do the work that follows.
